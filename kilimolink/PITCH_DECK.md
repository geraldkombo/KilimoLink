# KilimoLink Direct — I4C26 Pitch Deck

## Positioning

**KilimoLink is Nairobi's AI food system climate intelligence platform.**  
The marketplace is the data engine. The county dashboard is the product.

---

## Slide 1 — The One User: Keeping Nairobi Fed During Climate Shocks

**Judging criteria:** Presentation, Thematic relevance

- Our user is Nairobi City County's food system resilience officer.
- She must plan for food access, climate disruption, waste, and price shocks.
- Today she has no real-time data on where food comes from, where it goes, or where climate will break the chain.

**Visual:** Food resilience officer + Nairobi map with missing data gaps.

---

## Slide 2 — The Climate Food Risk

**Judging criteria:** Thematic relevance, Impact, Bonus: informal settlements

- Nairobi's food supply depends on routes from surrounding counties.
- Climate shocks disrupt roads, harvests, prices, and availability.
- Food waste creates methane; long routes increase transport emissions.
- Kibera, Mathare, and Mukuru face the sharpest food price and access shocks.

**Visual:** Flood/rain corridor → market price spike → informal settlement household.

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution

**Judging criteria:** Innovation, Thematic relevance

- KilimoLink Direct connects farmers directly with Nairobi buyers.
- Every transaction captures origin, destination, price, route, quantity, and product.
- That marketplace data becomes Nairobi's live food system sensor network.

**Visual:** Farmer listing → buyer order → county dashboard data stream.

---

## Slide 4 — County Climate Intelligence Dashboard

**Judging criteria:** Innovation, Impact, Presentation

- Live food-flow map.
- Price anomaly heatmap by neighborhood.
- Supply disruption alerts from weather + transaction signals.
- Food desert index for areas with low fresh produce access.
- CO₂e and food waste impact tiles.

**Visual:** Dashboard mock/prototype with four panels.

---

## Slide 5 — The AI Engines

**Judging criteria:** Innovation, Impact

1. **Supply disruption predictor:** forecasts crop shortages by route and neighborhood.
2. **Price anomaly detector:** flags unusual food inflation, especially in informal settlements.
3. **Carbon calculator:** estimates per-order emissions savings from shorter routes and avoided waste.

**Visual:** Three AI cards connected to dashboard alerts.

---

## Slide 6 — Why KilimoLink Is Different

**Judging criteria:** Innovation, Presentation

| Alternative | What they do | KilimoLink difference |
|---|---|---|
| Twiga Foods | B2B distribution | City climate intelligence from direct food-flow data |
| DigiFarm | Farmer services | Nairobi market access + county resilience dashboard |
| M-Farm | Price information | Live transactions + predictive alerts |
| Generic marketplace | Connects buyers and sellers | Marketplace-as-data-engine for government climate action |

**Key line:** Competitors move food. KilimoLink helps Nairobi understand, predict, and climate-proof its food system.
