# D-Prize Global Challenge Application Content
**Deadline:** 17 May 2026 | **Target:** $20,000

## 1. Which proven poverty-ending technology are you distributing?
We are distributing a **Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) Hyperlocal Marketplace** for climate-resilient agriculture.

## 2. Your Mission (1-sentence)
To reduce urban food poverty by 30% in informal settlements through a geolocation-aware exchange that eliminates middle-man costs and food waste.

## 3. The 3-Month Pilot Plan
- **Month 1**: Onboard 100 informal farmers/vendors in the Nairobi East area using our "Invisible Web3" (Privy) system.
- **Month 2**: Facilitate 500+ transactions within a 5km radius using our geolocation engine to prove "Food Mile" reduction.
- **Month 3**: Use the collected transaction data to provide the first "Digital Credit Score" for 50 vendors, enabling them to access future micro-financing.

## 4. Why You?
Our team combines "boots-on-the-ground" Nairobi strategy (Gerald Kombo) with a high-end technical stack (NestJS/Solana/DeepSeek). We aren't building a "theoretical" app; we have a functional prototype already mapping Nairobi’s informal food economy.

## 5. Scalability
Once the Nairobi pilot is proven, KilimoLink’s "Sub-City Grid" model can be deployed in any rapidly urbanizing city in the Global South (Lagos, Dhaka, Jakarta) with minimal re-configuration.

## 6. Budget Breakdown
- **Pilot Operations**: $8,000
- **Farmer Incentives (First 100)**: $5,000
- **Tech Maintenance & SMS Gateway**: $4,000
- **Monitoring & Evaluation**: $3,000
