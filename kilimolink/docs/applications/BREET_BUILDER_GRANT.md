# Breet Builder Grant Application Content
**Deadline:** 31 May 2026 | **Target:** $10,000

## 1. Project Name
**KilimoLink: Invisible Web3 for Informal Food Resilience**

## 2. Executive Summary (150 words)
KilimoLink is a hyperlocal marketplace designed to bridge the "Informal Settlement Gap" in Nairobi. By combining geolocation-aware logistics with a seamless "Invisible Web3" identity system (Privy), we enable informal farmers and vendors to build a verifiable digital transaction history without the friction of traditional blockchain onboarding. Our platform reduces food waste and transport emissions by prioritizing trade within a 5km radius, directly serving the urban poor while building a scalable data layer for climate-resilient cities.

## 3. What problem are you solving?
In Nairobi’s informal settlements, farmers and vendors operate in a "data desert." They have no verifiable credit history, are vulnerable to middle-man price exploitation, and suffer from high post-harvest losses due to inefficient logistics. Existing Web3 solutions fail them because of complex seed phrase management and high gas fees.

## 4. How does your solution work? (The Technical Edge)
KilimoLink uses a "Web2.5" stack:
- **Identity**: Privy allows users to create a Solana-compatible wallet using just their email, eliminating the need for seed phrases.
- **Logistics**: A Leaflet-powered geolocation engine maps hyper-local food sources.
- **AI Oracle**: DeepSeek-V3 provides real-time market pricing to protect against gouging.
- **Impact**: Recharts visualizes CO2 and waste reduction for city-level reporting.

## 5. Why Breet?
Breet focuses on user-centric innovation. KilimoLink is the ultimate test case for Breet’s mandate: taking complex decentralized technology and making it invisible and valuable to the world’s most vulnerable entrepreneurs.
