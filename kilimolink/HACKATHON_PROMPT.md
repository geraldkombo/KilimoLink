# KilimoLink Direct — I4C26 Winning Frame

## The visceral opening

It is raining in Nairobi. A truck carrying tomatoes from Kiambu is stuck on a flooded road. By evening, prices rise in Mathare. By morning, a mother in Mukuru skips fresh vegetables because the market price doubled. At Dandora, unsold food rots and releases methane. At City Hall, the resilience officer responsible for food access has no live map, no alert, and no data showing where the food chain is breaking.

That is the problem KilimoLink solves.

---

## One-line definition

**KilimoLink is Nairobi's AI food system climate intelligence platform.**  
The marketplace is the data engine. The county dashboard is the product.

---

## Focus test

- **One city:** Nairobi, Kenya.
- **One user:** Nairobi City County food system resilience officer.
- **One problem:** The city has no real-time food-flow, price-risk, waste, and climate disruption intelligence.
- **One solution:** A working marketplace that generates live data for an AI-powered county dashboard.

---

## Why this is city climate action

KilimoLink connects food access, emissions, waste, and climate adaptation:

- Shorter supply chains reduce transport emissions.
- Faster direct sales reduce spoilage and landfill methane.
- Price anomaly detection protects vulnerable households from climate-linked food shocks.
- Supply disruption alerts help the county act before shortages hit.
- Food desert mapping shows where fresh produce access is weakest.

---

## Why this is AI-driven

KilimoLink uses three practical AI/intelligence engines that can run on free-tier infrastructure:

1. **Supply disruption predictor**  
   Uses weather forecasts, route risk, seasonality, and transaction trends to warn which crops and neighborhoods may face shortages.

2. **Price anomaly detector**  
   Tracks product prices by neighborhood and flags abnormal spikes, especially in Kibera, Mathare, and Mukuru.

3. **Carbon impact calculator**  
   Estimates per-order emissions savings from reduced food miles and avoided waste, then aggregates impact for the county dashboard.

---

## Informal settlements bonus track

KilimoLink prioritizes Kibera, Mathare, and Mukuru because climate shocks hit informal settlements first and hardest.

The platform helps by:

- monitoring food price spikes by neighborhood;
- identifying areas with low fresh produce access;
- routing nearby farmers and sellers toward underserved communities;
- warning county teams before rain, road disruption, or supply drops become household crises.

---

## Why KilimoLink is different

A generic AI idea would be: "connect farmers to consumers."

KilimoLink is different because it turns a marketplace into a climate intelligence layer for Nairobi County. Competitors may move food, show prices, or support farmers. KilimoLink helps the city understand, predict, and climate-proof its food system.

This comes from lived experience. The team is based in Nairobi and understands the market routes, middlemen, food inflation, and neighborhoods that are usually invisible in official data.
