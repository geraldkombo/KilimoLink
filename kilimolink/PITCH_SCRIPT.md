## 0:00–0:35 - The Hook

> "Today, Nairobi's food resilience officer has no real-time view of where food is coming from, which routes carry it, which neighborhoods face price spikes, or where climate disruption will hit next."

## 0:35–1:05 - The Climate Problem + Transport Angle

> "Nairobi depends on food trucked in from Kiambu, Machakos, Kajiado, and beyond. Every kilometer adds emissions, every middleman adds cost, and every empty truck returning from the city is wasted fuel. When heavy rain, drought, road disruption, or fuel prices hit that chain, the impact lands first in Kibera, Mathare, and Mukuru. Food waste becomes methane. Long supply routes create emissions. And price shocks decide whether families eat fresh food or skip meals. This is not only a food problem. It is urban climate risk - and it is a transport problem."

**[Slide: Food transport routes map → flooded road → market price spike → informal settlement household]**

---

## 1:05–1:45 - The Solution + Marketplace Demo

> "We built KilimoLink Direct: climate-smart food transport intelligence. The marketplace is the data engine - but it is also a smarter logistics network. A farmer lists sukuma wiki, tomatoes, or potatoes. A buyer in a demand zone like Mathare orders directly. Every transaction records origin, destination, price, quantity, and route. That creates the real-time food-flow and transport data Nairobi does not have today."

**[Live demo: open marketplace → product listing → sell flow or product detail]**

> "On screen, this looks like a farmer-to-consumer marketplace. But for Nairobi's food resilience officer, every listing becomes a sensor for the city's food system - tracking which corridors have supply, where demand is unmet, and how to reduce empty trips and last-mile waste."

---

## 1:45–2:25 - County Dashboard + AI

> "Now the county sees the real product: a dashboard showing food transport flows into Nairobi, which corridors face climate risk, price spikes by neighborhood, informal settlements at risk, and food deserts with too few active sellers nearby. Three AI engines power it: a supply disruption predictor using weather, route, and transaction patterns; a price anomaly detector for sudden inflation in vulnerable wards; and a carbon calculator estimating emissions saved per order through shorter routes, fewer middlemen, and less waste."

**[Live demo: open `/county-dashboard` with map, heatmap, alert, CO₂ tile]**

## 2:25–2:45 - Why We Win

> "This is different from Twiga, DigiFarm, or a generic marketplace. They move food or support farmers. KilimoLink gives the city climate intelligence - and smarter food logistics. We match supply zones to demand neighborhoods, reduce empty truck trips, cut middlemen, and shorten last-mile routes. We are Nairobi-based developers building from lived experience: we know the markets, the middlemen, the matatu routes, and the neighborhoods that get ignored."
