### **Elevator Pitch (The "30-Second Hook")**
KilimoLink is a hyperlocal, geolocation-aware marketplace that bridges the gap between informal settlement farmers and urban consumers. By using "Invisible Web3" for identity and AI for fair pricing, we eliminate food waste and transport emissions, building climate-resilient cities from the ground up.

---

### **The "Why Now?" (Urgency)**
By 2050, 60% of Africa will live in cities. Current food systems are centralized, carbon-heavy, and exclude the informal poor. KilimoLink is the first functional "Urban Social Ecology" platform that turns informal settlements into sustainable food hubs.

---

### **Technical Specifications (The "How It's Built")**
- **Backend**: NestJS (Node.js) with Prisma ORM and SQLite/PostgreSQL.
- **Frontend**: React + Vite + Material UI for a high-performance, mobile-responsive UI.
- **Mapping**: Leaflet.js with custom geolocation logic for 5km-radius trading.
- **Intelligence**: DeepSeek-V3 AI model for real-time market price stabilization.
- **Web3**: Privy (Privy-io) for "Keyless" wallet management and Solana for immutable transaction logs.

---

### **Impact Metrics (The "What We Track")**
1. **Food Miles Saved**: KM reduced by sourcing hyper-locally.
2. **Post-Harvest Loss**: Percentage of produce saved from waste via instant marketplace matches.
3. **Digital Inclusion**: Number of informal vendors with a verifiable on-chain identity.
4. **Income Growth**: Average increase in profit for farmers by removing middlemen.
