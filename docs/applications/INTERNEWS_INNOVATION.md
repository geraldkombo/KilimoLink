## 1. Project Title
**The Price Transparency Oracle: Fighting Information Poverty in Urban Food Systems**

## 2. Problem Statement
In informal settlements, "Information Poverty" is as dangerous as food poverty. Market prices are often manipulated by middlemen, and during climate shocks (droughts/floods), misinformation leads to panic buying and price gouging. Vulnerable urban consumers and small-scale farmers lack a "single source of truth" for fair market value.

## 3. Innovation Description
KilimoLink implements an **AI-Driven Fair Trade Oracle**. Using the **DeepSeek-V3** model, the platform ingests official market data (KNBS/NDMA) and real-time transaction data from our hyperlocal marketplace. It then broadcasts "Fair Price Guidance" to users via a simple web interface.

## 4. Journalism & Information Component
This project serves as a "Citizen Information Hub." We aren't just a marketplace; we are a data platform that empowers informal vendors with the same level of market intelligence that large-scale commercial farms possess. Our Impact Dashboard provides journalists and city planners with verifiable data on food availability and pricing trends in "blind spot" areas like Kibera and Mathare.

## 5. Use of Funds
- **Local Data Mapping**: Hiring youth "Resilience Officers" to verify local food sources ($10k).
- **API Optimization**: Scaling our DeepSeek oracle to handle high-frequency pricing updates ($10k).
- **Community Outreach**: Training informal vendor cooperatives on how to use the "Price Truth" dashboard ($10k).
